Fire Extinguisher Service

CGS provides professional fire extinguisher supply, installation, servicing, and maintenance across Cornwall, fully compliant with BS 5306 standards.

We offer a complete end-to-end service tailored to homes and businesses of all sizes.

Qualified & Accredited

CGS is fully qualified and a member of the Fire Protection Association (FPA).

CGS Cornwall have:

  • Passed the BAFE fire extinguisher examination at the National Fire Service College (Moreton-in-Marsh)
  • Completed a refresher course in May 2026
  • Level 3 qualification in Fire Risk Assessment 

Free Site Survey & Advice

CGS provide a free on-site survey and quotation service, including advice on the correct type and number of extinguishers required for your premises.

No obligation, no pressure.
